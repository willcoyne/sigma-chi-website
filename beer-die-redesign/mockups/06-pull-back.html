Friendship

A bond built on genuine loyalty rather than convenience — the belief that brothers are stronger, and better men, together than alone.

02

Justice

A commitment to fairness and integrity in how members treat one another and the wider community — doing right even when no one is watching.

We're Building Something New

In spring 2026, a new class of founding fathers began forming Sigma Chi's colony at WashU. Once it is rechartered, the colony will become the Tau Tau chapter again.

The founding class is focused, in the words of our expansion coordinator, on "community service, more academics, and more philanthropy."
