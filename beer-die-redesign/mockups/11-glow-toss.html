The Three Great Aims

What We Stand For

In Hoc Signo Vinces. Three aims, set down in 1855 at Miami University, and the ones this colony is being built on.

AIM 01

Friendship

A bond built on genuine loyalty rather than convenience — the belief that brothers are stronger, and better men, together than alone.

AIM 02

Justice

A commitment to fairness and integrity in how members treat one another and the wider community — doing right even when no one is watching.

AIM 03

Learning

A dedication to scholarship and growth, in and out of the classroom, as a lifelong pursuit rather than a college requirement.

We're Building Something New

In spring 2026, a new class of founding fathers began forming Sigma Chi's colony at WashU. Once it is rechartered, the colony will become the Tau Tau chapter again. The founding class is focused, in the words of our expansion coordinator, on "community service, more academics, and more philanthropy."
